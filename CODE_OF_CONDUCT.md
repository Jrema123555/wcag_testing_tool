# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Respect privacy and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publication of private information are unacceptable in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and events.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and account for context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a private contact listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish private incident details in an issue. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially and avoid participating in enforcement where they have a conflict of interest.
